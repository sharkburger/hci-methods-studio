# Reading note

Name:
Paper (full citation and link):

1. **Question:** What does the paper investigate, and why does it matter?
2. **Claim:** State the central argument in your own words.
3. **Evidence:** Locate one figure, excerpt, observation, or result supporting it. Include page/section.
4. **Method:** How was evidence generated and interpreted? What assumptions follow?
5. **Limits:** What does this evidence not establish? Who or what is outside its scope?
6. **Discussion:** Ask a question that admits competing interpretations.
7. **Transfer:** How might this method inform your own question? What would need to change?
8. **Reflection:** What changed after discussion?

AI/tool disclosure, if used: tool, purpose, output checked, source verification.
Do not include identifiable participant or confidential research information.
