# Sources and editorial provenance

## Reference syllabus and scope

The core follows [Christopher A. Le Dantec’s INAM 7001: Research Methods in Interdisciplinary Design & Media, Fall 2026](https://ledantec.net/teaching/inam-7001-fall-2026/). The reference page’s article and schedule were inspected directly.

The site preserves all fifteen calendar positions, including two breaks, a poster session, and final submission; all seventy-four reading/resource entries in READ, STRETCH, and DO order; the original milestones and guest context; and the source assessment weights. Reference dates and guests are not new course announcements or invitations.

The method notes, practical approaches, outputs, limitations, extension activities, and teaching aids are newly authored. They organize a broad methods overview rather than claiming a complete taxonomy of HCI. This site does not reproduce the source’s institutional policy prose or unpublished assignment handouts. The source page displays a [CC BY-NC 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/); its authored material retains that license. Bibliographic facts and links are recorded with attribution and are not presented as this site author’s original scholarship.

## Link and citation provenance

Twelve embedded reading destinations are retained exactly as observed in the source’s weekly table. The Oxford Bibliographies material-culture URL and Asaro DOI, printed as text in that table, are retained too. Other article/footer destinations are preserved as source context. See [SOURCE_LINKS.md](SOURCE_LINKS.md).

The original Swann PDF uses HTTP; its exact source URL is preserved. Original-link inclusion does not guarantee continued availability, HTTPS support, or open full-text access. No destination has been silently replaced with a guessed DOI.

When the source gives no link, a separately located author/publisher page is labelled **Added publication / author link**. Otherwise, **Publication lookup** leads to a Crossref discovery query using the citation text. This is not a verified record or a full-text link. Incomplete authors, dates, or editions remain explicit. Minor typography/capitalization is normalized; source ambiguities such as Keshav’s listed year and the two Vaughn entries are marked rather than silently reconciled.

All entries have been catalogued; the full source reading corpus has **not** been read exhaustively. Method-level notes and teaching prompts are not represented as summaries of every assigned chapter or paper.

## Personalization

The user specified English, an NYU ITP direction, and health. [Binyan Xu’s public ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0007-7387-2407) supplied the name and bibliographic metadata for four selected personal works. [NYU ITP/IMA](https://tisch.nyu.edu/itp) and [ITP Physical Computing](https://itp.nyu.edu/physcomp/) supply context for creative technology practice. No institutional endorsement or current appointment is implied.

Personal works are linked by DOI. Titles, contributors, and dates were checked against ORCID. Their full texts were not all reviewed. Questions about them are teaching prompts, not findings or claims of clinical effectiveness.

The six interest-led routes and eighteen additional entries are visibly distinct from the reference reading assignments. PRISMA is presented as a reporting guideline, and the CONSORT pilot extension is scoped to randomized pilot/feasibility trials.

## Visual design and data

The original CSS composition draws on De Stijl’s orthogonal lines, asymmetry, and primary colors; it does not reproduce a specific painting. Typography is informed by the reference course’s bold blue italic headings and monospaced body.

The site has no accounts, submission backend, tracking scripts, or participant-record storage. Filters run in the browser. External links have their own data practices. Do not commit sensitive participant or health records.
