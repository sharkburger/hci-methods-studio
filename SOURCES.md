# Sources and editorial provenance

## Course architecture

Christopher A. Le Dantec’s [INAM 7001: Research Methods in Interdisciplinary Design & Media, Fall 2026](https://ledantec.net/teaching/inam-7001-fall-2026/) informed the course architecture: objectives, methods-based weekly reading, core/stretch reading distinctions, student facilitation, staged projects, and a final individual research proposal.

This studio uses an original 12-week sequence, original annotations and exercises, an adapted assessment model, and a separately drafted AI policy. It does not reproduce the reference course’s syllabus prose, assignment handouts, university policies, or full articles. The reference page was reviewed; its linked reading corpus was not exhaustively read. Its broad syllabus is adapted toward creative technology, embodiment, and health.

## Personalization

[Binyan Xu’s public ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0009-0007-7387-2407) supplied the name and bibliographic metadata for the selected personal works. The user specified English, the NYU ITP direction, and health. [NYU ITP/IMA](https://tisch.nyu.edu/itp) provides context for a creative technology studio tradition. No institutional endorsement or current appointment is implied.

Four personal works are linked by DOI. Their titles, contributors, and dates were checked against the ORCID record. Full texts were not all reviewed. The site explicitly presents questions about these works as **teaching prompts**, not findings or method summaries. Do not infer efficacy, participant characteristics, or research outcomes from a title.

## Teaching and site design

The studio cycle and proposed assessment are editorial teaching choices. ICAP informs the choice to ask learners to explain, compare, and revise; no causal claim about this course’s outcomes has been established. Decorative diagrams are original CSS artwork and are not scientific models.

The site contains no student accounts, submission backend, tracking scripts, or participant data storage. Search and filters run in the browser. GitHub and linked external sites have their own data practices.

## Updating this resource

For each added paper, verify title, authors, year, and primary link; distinguish a completed close reading from a metadata-based recommendation. Keep quotations within permissions and do not upload paywalled PDFs. Add the source and editorial rationale for substantial adaptations.
